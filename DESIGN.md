# DESIGN.md — Bani Adam Afandi Portfolio

## Identity
- **Name:** Bani Adam Afandi
- **Role:** Product Owner & Software Quality Engineer
- **Personality:** Technical, precise, data-driven, no-nonsense
- **Audience:** Tech recruiters, potential clients, fellow developers

## Personality
- Direct and factual
- Technical depth without jargon overload
- Professional but approachable
- Evidence-based claims only

## Palette
- **Primary Dark:** #0a0f0d (deep forest green-black)
- **Primary Light:** #f8faf9 (off-white with green tint)
- **Accent:** #6ee7b7 (muted mint green)
- **Text Dark:** #e8f2ec (light green-white)
- **Text Light:** #1a2e23 (dark green-black)
- **Muted:** #9db3a6 (sage green)
- **Borders:** #1f2a24 (dark), #d4e0d8 (light)

## Typography
- **Headings:** Geist Sans (clean, modern, not trendy)
- **Body:** Geist Sans
- **Mono:** Geist Mono (for code/technical terms only)
- **Reason:** Geist is functional and readable, fits technical portfolio

## Mood
- Professional engineering portfolio
- Not a marketing landing page
- Content-first, not decoration-first
- Subtle animations that guide, not distract

## Dials
- **ENERGY:** 2 (calm, focused)
- **RHYTHM:** 2 (consistent with subtle variation)
- **MOTION:** 1 (minimal, purposeful only)

## Anti-Slop Rules Applied
- No em dashes in UI text (use commas, periods, colons)
- No generic AI icons (Lucide OK for functional icons only)
- No glassmorphism on navbar
- No excessive gradients
- No fake statistics or testimonials
- No generic CTAs ("Get Started", "Learn More")
- No bento grid layouts
- No uniform card grids
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Keyboard accessible
- Real data only
